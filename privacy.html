Privacy Policy

Simple Schedule

Effective 10 November 2026 (revised for version 1.1: private iCloud sync between your iPhones). The previous version, effective 27 September 2026, applies to versions 1.0 to 1.0.2, which keep everything on one iPhone.

HomeSupport

The short version. Simple Schedule has no account system and no server. Your templates and schedule are stored on your iPhone and, if you're signed in to iCloud, synced to your other iPhones through your own private iCloud account, which Arosemena Apps cannot read. Nothing is collected about you, nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising. Besides your iCloud account, the only place your schedule goes is the calendar you choose to publish it to.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage area. Nothing is uploaded to Arosemena Apps, and there is no Arosemena Apps service to upload it to.

When you're signed in to iCloud, the items marked synced are also kept in your private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), so the same schedule appears on your other iPhones. That data belongs to your Apple Account: it is stored and protected by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, it counts toward your iCloud storage, and Arosemena Apps has no access to it. If you're not signed in to iCloud, or you turn iCloud off for Simple Schedule in Settings › [your name] › iCloud, everything stays on that iPhone and the app works as before.

Calendar access

Simple Schedule asks for Calendar access only when you first publish a schedule or set up calendars in Settings. With it, the app:

  • Shows events from the calendars you choose (for example, a work schedule synced to your iPhone), so you can see which days are already taken.
  • Publishes the days you schedule to the calendar you choose, and updates or removes those same events when your schedule changes.
  • Lets you edit or delete an event yourself, in Apple's standard event editor. Simple Schedule never changes your other events on its own.

Your calendar events are read on your iPhone only. Your other events are never copied into Simple Schedule's storage or iCloud, and nothing from your calendars is sent anywhere by the app. The events Simple Schedule published sync between your devices through your calendar accounts, as they always have. Each iPhone asks for its own Calendar access and publishes only when you tap Publish on it.

Calendars from Google, Exchange, and other accounts are the ones you have added to your iPhone in Settings. Those accounts sync the events on their own, under their own terms. Simple Schedule never sees your passwords for them and never contacts those services directly.

Calendar access is optional. Without it, your schedule still works in the app, and you can export a month as an .ics file and put it wherever you like.

Location

When you type a location for a template, Simple Schedule suggests addresses and places using Apple Maps. What you type is sent to Apple to find matches, under Apple's privacy policy; it is not sent to Arosemena Apps.

Location access is optional and off until you tap Show Places Near Me. If you allow it, your approximate location is used on your iPhone, only to show nearby places first. It is not saved and not sent anywhere by Simple Schedule. Declining changes nothing else, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, except your synced schedule data in your own private iCloud account (described above), the calendar events you publish, the location text you type (looked up with Apple Maps, as described above), and any .ics file you choose to share. There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting that transmits your content, and no remote configuration. The app contains no third-party code.

Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Arosemena Apps receives no payment details. Tips unlock nothing, and the app does not record whether you have tipped.

Backups

Simple Schedule's data is included in your iPhone's normal backup, whether that is iCloud or a computer. Those backups are managed by Apple under Apple's terms, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone only. Your synced schedule stays in your iCloud account for your other iPhones. To remove it everywhere, either delete your templates and erase your days in Simple Schedule while signed in to iCloud (the deletions sync), or delete Simple Schedule's data from your iCloud storage in Settings › [your name] › iCloud, where iOS lists the apps using your storage.

Calendar events Simple Schedule published stay in your calendar until you delete them. You can remove them first by erasing the days in Simple Schedule and tapping Update Calendar.

Children

Simple Schedule is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy
