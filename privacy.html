Privacy Policy

Simple Schedule

Effective 27 September 2026

HomeSupport

The short version. Simple Schedule has no account system and no server. Your shifts and schedule are stored on your iPhone. Nothing is collected about you, nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising. The only place your schedule goes is the calendar on your iPhone that you choose to publish it to.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage area. It is not uploaded to Arosemena Apps, and there is no service to upload it to.

InformationWhy the app has it
Shifts you create: name, times, location, notes, color, alertSo you can place them on your schedule
The days you scheduleYour monthly schedule
Which calendar events Simple Schedule created, and the calendar you choseSo it can update or remove its own events later without creating duplicates
Small preferences, such as the last month you viewedSo the app opens where you left it

Calendar access

Simple Schedule asks for Calendar access only when you first publish a schedule. It uses that access to add the shifts you publish to the calendar you choose, and to update or remove those same events when your schedule changes.

iOS grants this as full access, because adding an event without it does not allow the app to update or remove the event later. Simple Schedule does not display, import, copy, or change any other event in your calendars, and nothing from your calendars is sent anywhere by the app.

Calendars from Google, Exchange, and other accounts are the ones you have added to your iPhone in Settings. Those accounts sync the events on their own, under their own terms. Simple Schedule never sees your passwords for them and never contacts those services directly.

Calendar access is optional. Without it, your schedule still works in the app, and you can export a month as an .ics file and put it wherever you like.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, except the calendar events you publish (as described above) and any .ics file you choose to share. There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting that transmits your content, and no remote configuration. The app contains no third-party code.

Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Arosemena Apps receives no payment details. Tips unlock nothing, and the app does not record whether you have tipped.

Backups

Simple Schedule's data is included in your iPhone's normal backup, whether that is iCloud or a computer. Those backups are managed by Apple under Apple's terms, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. Calendar events it published stay in your calendar until you delete them. You can remove them first by erasing the days in Simple Schedule and tapping Update Calendar.

Children

Simple Schedule is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and the previous version stays in this site's history on GitHub.
