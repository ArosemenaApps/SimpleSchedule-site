Simple Schedule

Paint your shifts. Sync your calendar.

Privacy Policy Support

Simple Schedule is for people whose work schedule changes every week. Define your usual shifts once, tap or drag them onto the days you work, and publish the month into the calendar you already use: iCloud, Google, or any calendar set up on your iPhone.

How it works

  • Create your usual shifts: name, times, location, color, and an optional alert. Overnight shifts are supported.
  • Pick a shift and tap, or drag across, the days you work.
  • Tap Publish to Calendar. Change a day later and tap Update Calendar: the same event is updated, never duplicated.
  • Prefer not to connect Calendar? Export any month as an .ics file instead.

What it does not do

No account. No sign-in. No subscription. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Simple Schedule is free, with optional tips that unlock nothing.
