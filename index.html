Simple Schedule

Paint your month. Sync your calendar.

Privacy Policy Support

Simple Schedule is for the things you repeat, but not on a neat pattern: work shifts, classes, the gym, school pickup. Define each one once as a template, tap or drag it onto the days it happens, and publish the month into the calendar you already use: iCloud, Google, or any calendar set up on your iPhone.

How it works

  • Create templates: name, times, location (with address suggestions), color, and an optional alert. Overnight times are supported.
  • Pick a template and tap, or drag across, the days it happens.
  • See what's already on your calendar, such as a synced work schedule, right on the month, and edit or delete those events.
  • Tap Publish to Calendar. Change a day later and tap Update Calendar: the same event is updated, never duplicated.
  • Prefer not to connect Calendar? Export any month as an .ics file instead.

What it does not do

No account. No sign-in. No subscription. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Simple Schedule is free, with optional tips that unlock nothing.
